# DSH Remote Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

DSH Remote is an independent open-source companion for controlling a Harness instance running on a computer you own or manage. The project maintainer does not operate a relay, analytics, advertising, account, or telemetry service for the iOS app.

## Data handled by the app

The app stores the display name and network address of computers you add in iOS local preferences. For a same-Wi-Fi pairing, it also stores the random access credential generated by Desktop. It reads task titles, conversation messages, tool summaries, task status, approval requests, and questions directly from your computer. It sends prompts, stop requests, approval decisions, and question answers directly to that computer.

The app does not send this content to the project maintainer. The Harness instance on your computer may send prompts and related context to the model provider you configured there. That transfer is governed by your provider configuration and the provider's privacy terms, not by a DSH Remote service.

## Camera, notifications, and local network

Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a pairing QR code. The app does not save camera images. Local-network access is used to reach a Harness computer on your network. Notifications are generated on the device when a connected task completes or needs your attention.

## Optional network services

You may use Tailscale or another HTTPS setup you control to make your computer reachable. On a trusted private Wi-Fi, you may instead enable Desktop's authenticated LAN endpoint. That LAN endpoint is not encrypted and should not be used on untrusted networks. DSH Remote does not receive Tailscale credentials. Tailscale and any model provider are separate services with their own terms and privacy policies.

## Retention and deletion

Task history remains on your computer. Saved computer names, addresses, and any LAN access credentials remain on the iPhone until you remove them. You can delete individual computers from the main list or delete all saved computers from About & Privacy.

## Tracking and advertising

DSH Remote contains no advertising SDK and does not track users across apps or websites.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, open an issue at <https://github.com/chokwinlee/deepseek-harness-desktop/issues>.
